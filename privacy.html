What information do we collect?

The enquiry form asks for limited contact details and broad business information such as industry, region, years trading, employee range, turnover range, profitability range, owner involvement, reason for considering a transition and likely timeframe. Do not request or submit sensitive personal information or detailed business records through the initial form.

Why do we collect it?

To assess and respond to an enquiry about a possible business acquisition, contact the person who submitted it, maintain enquiry records, and take steps requested by the enquirer before any potential transaction.

Who may receive it?

Information may be processed by Google services used to operate the form and spreadsheet. If the enquiry progresses, information should only be shared with professional advisers or other recipients where appropriate, with suitable notice or permission and safeguards. Identify any additional providers before launch.

Storage, access and retention

Restrict spreadsheet access to authorised people, use strong account security and multi-factor authentication, and set a retention period. Delete enquiries that are no longer required, subject to legal recordkeeping obligations. Do not place the response spreadsheet on a public link.

Your choices and questions

Provide a contact method for access, correction, deletion requests and privacy complaints. Explain how complaints are handled and any applicable rights. Do not claim the Privacy Act does or does not apply until the entity's circumstances have been checked.

Marketing

Do not add enquiry contacts to unrelated marketing lists automatically. Any optional marketing consent must be separate, voluntary and unticked by default. Comply with applicable spam and privacy rules.
