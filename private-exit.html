Why some owners prefer a private conversation

A public sale campaign can be appropriate for some businesses. For others, it may feel premature or disruptive. An initial private discussion can help you decide whether to explore a transaction at all.

Concerns owners commonly raise

  • Employees may hear about a sale before the owner is ready.
  • Customers or suppliers may misinterpret a public listing.
  • Competitors may learn commercially sensitive information.
  • The owner may not know what a realistic transition involves.
  • The owner may want to test interest before deciding on next steps.

What “private” means here

We use the information you submit to assess and respond to your enquiry. We do not publish your enquiry as a listing. Access should be limited to people who need it to handle the enquiry. If a potential transaction progresses, further information should only be shared with your permission and under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

Important: No website or business can promise absolute secrecy. Information may need to be handled by service providers or professional advisers, and legal obligations may apply. Read the Privacy page before submitting.
