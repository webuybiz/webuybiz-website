Acquisition interests

Established businesses with a future beyond the current owner.

We are interested in learning about established Australian businesses where there may be a sensible ownership transition.

Areas of interest

Our interests may evolve as we assess opportunities, available capital and operational fit. These are areas of interest, not a promise to acquire every business submitted.

Hospitality

Cafes, food businesses and established hospitality operations with transferable systems and customer demand.

Automotive

Repair, servicing and related businesses with repeat customers and capable teams.

Wholesale & distribution

Established B2B supply operations with repeat accounts and reliable processes.

Trade services

Service businesses with established customer relationships, contracts and delivery capability.

Manufacturing

Small and medium manufacturers with sound operations, equipment and customer relationships.

Other established businesses

We may consider other sectors where the business model, financials and transition opportunity make sense.

What makes an opportunity worth exploring?

We look beyond turnover alone. A sustainable business needs customers, sound records, a workable operating model and a realistic transition plan.

  • Trading history and verifiable financial information.
  • Clear products, services and customer proposition.
  • Reasonable understanding of owner dependence.
  • Employees, licences, leases and contracts that can be assessed.
  • Realistic seller expectations and willingness to discuss due diligence.
We do not provide a valuation or acquisition offer based only on this form. Any assessment is preliminary and subject to appropriate financial, legal, tax, operational and commercial due diligence.
