Considering a sale now or in the future?

That's okay. Some owners are exploring possibilities 6–24 months ahead. Others simply want to understand whether a transition could be realistic.

  • You want to retire but have no family successor.
  • You are ready for a different chapter after years of running the business.
  • You want to reduce your involvement or explore a staged transition.
  • You prefer not to alert employees, customers or competitors prematurely.
  • You are curious about a potential sale but have not made a decision.
