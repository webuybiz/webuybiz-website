A measured process

Start with a conversation. Progress only if it makes sense.

There is no need to prepare a sale pack before making an initial enquiry.

01

Initial enquiry

You share broad business information and your preferred way to be contacted. Avoid confidential details and documents.

02

Introductory discussion

If appropriate, we contact you to understand your objectives, timing and the broad shape of the business.

03

Preliminary review

We may ask for further high-level information to understand whether an acquisition could be viable.

04

Confidentiality arrangements

Before sensitive information is exchanged, appropriate confidentiality and information-sharing arrangements should be agreed.

05

Due diligence & terms

If both sides wish to proceed, professional advisers can review financial, legal, tax, employment, lease and operational matters.

06

Formal documentation

Any acquisition proceeds only under definitive signed legal documents and applicable approvals.

What happens after you enquire?

We review the information you provide and, if the opportunity fits our current interests, contact you using the details supplied. We may not be able to progress every enquiry.

There is no automatic commitment

  • Submitting the form is not an offer to sell.
  • It is not an offer to buy or a promise of funding.
  • No valuation is guaranteed.
  • You are not required to provide documents at the enquiry stage.
  • You should obtain independent legal, tax and financial advice before a transaction.
