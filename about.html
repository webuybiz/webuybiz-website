A considered approach to ownership transition

Respect for what you've built. Care in what comes next.

WeBuyBiz is a proposed direct business acquisition brand. Its intended model is to assess and, where suitable, acquire established Australian businesses for its own account.

Our approach

We believe a business owner should be able to explore a possible transition without feeling pressured to launch a public sale campaign. We aim to listen first, understand the business and be clear about what would need to happen before any transaction could proceed.

What we value

  • Respect for owners, employees and customers.
  • Clear communication and realistic expectations.
  • Confidential handling of information.
  • Evidence-led assessment rather than promises.
  • Proper professional advice and documented processes.
